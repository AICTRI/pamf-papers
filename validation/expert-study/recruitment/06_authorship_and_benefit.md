# Authorship and Participant Benefit

Completing a rating workbook is research participation, not authorship. Raters
should not be added to the manuscript solely as compensation or recognition.
Doing so would also undermine the claim that their judgments were independent.

Recommended participant benefit:

- a fixed honorarium stated before participation and unrelated to ratings;
- a post-study result summary;
- optional acknowledgment by name only with explicit consent;
- optional public release of de-identified labels only with separate consent.

A participant may be considered for authorship only through a separate role
that includes substantial contribution to study conception or analysis,
manuscript drafting or critical revision, final approval, and accountability
for the work. Such a contributor must not be counted as a blinded independent
rater in the reported expert comparison.
