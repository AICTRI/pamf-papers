**Seeking experienced software architects for a short independent research study**

I am recruiting three experienced architects for an independent, unfunded
research study on architecture viewpoint prioritization. This is personal
research, not employer-sponsored work or product promotion.

Participants will independently review one fictional cross-border data-platform
scenario and classify 61 architecture viewpoints. The task is completed in an
Excel workbook, requires no coding, and takes approximately 30-45 minutes.

Eligibility:

- five or more years of professional experience as a software, solution,
  systems, or enterprise architect;
- architecture design or review work within the last two years;
- preferably experience in data, integration, security, compliance, or cloud
  architecture;
- no previous exposure to the decision model or manuscript being evaluated;
- ability to complete the assessment independently in English.

Each participant who submits a complete valid assessment will receive a fixed
US$75 net-equivalent honorarium. Compensation does not depend on the ratings or
agreement with any model. Responses will be pseudonymized and reported by
participant ID. Participation does not imply manuscript authorship.
