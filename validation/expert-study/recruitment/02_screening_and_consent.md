## Study Information

**Researcher:** Huiwen Han, independent researcher, hanhuiwen@gmail.com.

**Purpose:** To measure agreement among experienced architects and alignment
between their judgments and a configurable architecture-viewpoint decision
model.

**Procedure:** You will read one fictional benchmark scenario and classify 61
viewpoints as Mandatory, Recommended, or Optional. You will not receive the
model output until your responses are frozen. Expected time is 30-45 minutes.

**Data collected:** Anonymous participant ID, role category, experience range,
relevant domain experience, ratings, and brief rationales. Your LinkedIn name,
profile, and contact details are used only for recruitment and stored
separately from ratings.

**Publication and sharing:** Aggregate statistics and de-identified
disagreements may appear in a research manuscript. De-identified rating labels
may be released only if separately approved by participants. Contact details,
identity mappings, and free text that could identify a person will not be made
public.

**Risks:** The study asks for professional judgment on a fictional scenario and
does not request employer, client, project, health, financial, or other
sensitive information. Do not include confidential project examples in your
rationale.

**Benefits and compensation:** The fixed honorarium is US$75 net equivalent,
payable after a complete submission and independent of rating content. The
default method is a PayPal international commercial payment. The recipient's
country, receiving capability, currency, and any gross-up needed for fees will
be confirmed privately before enrollment. No payment account details are part
of the research dataset. Participants will receive a summary of results. No
direct professional benefit is promised.

**Voluntary participation:** Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any
time. You may request deletion of your submission until September 17, 2026.
After de-identification and aggregate analysis, individual withdrawal may no
longer be feasible.

**Authorship:** Completing the rating task does not qualify a participant as an
author. Participants who make a separate substantial contribution to study
design, analysis, manuscript drafting, final approval, and accountability may
be considered under normal authorship criteria, but cannot simultaneously be
reported as independent blinded raters.
