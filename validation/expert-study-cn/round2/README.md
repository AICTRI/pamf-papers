# Round 2: Priority-Budget Expert Study

The same three architects selected and ranked exactly 20 of 61 viewpoints on
two scenarios: `Case X` (saturated high risk) and `Case M` (moderate risk).
Forcing a priority budget produced higher agreement (binary Fleiss' kappa =
0.182; mean pairwise top-20 overlap 45%).

## Interpretation

This round tests whether a forced priority budget changes measured convergence
and whether that depends on the scenario. It does not create a ground truth and
does not establish that AVDM is correct. The within-subject, fixed-order design
cannot isolate task format from learning, memory, timing, scenario order, or
repeated exposure.
